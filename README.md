Juga Paazmaya is a design engineering leader, teacher and open-source maintainer based in Helsinki. His work sits where user experience, secure engineering and education meet. He began in 2009 prototyping interfaces for the Nokia N900 and N9. He then led front-end and UI architecture at Comptel, Tieto, Finnair and OP Financial Group, where he is now Application Security Researcher. There he works on making AI-assisted security tools part of the everyday development lifecycle.

Sharing knowledge runs through all of it. His teacher training at HAMK (2014) ended in a thesis on using GitHub as a central tool for an educational organisation. He taught Node.js at Metropolia in 2014 and has taught there again since 2022. His courses cover empathy, user-centred design, cybersecurity, WebAssembly and Rust, with real DevSecOps practice (GitHub, Playwright, CI/CD) built in. In industry he built a 100+ member design and engineering community of practice at OP. At Finnair he trained over 100 colleagues in accessibility, and at Comptel he mentored 50+ developers. His teaching is open code: template repositories, reference solutions, automated scoring and a move from GitHub Classroom to Classroom 50, all of which can be inspected.

His technical areas are:
- **Accessibility and design systems.** React and TypeScript at OP and Finnair, IAAP CPACC certified, and a Flutter design system built for Design Leaders Finland.
- **Security.** Application security and DevSecOps, with CISSP in progress and CompTIA Security+ and CCSK held.
- **Rust and WebAssembly.** Browser-side tools, Tauri apps and embedded firmware for ESP32 and nRF52840 boards.
- **Applied AI.** Local LoRA training, kanji recognition and speech pipelines. His MBA thesis (Turku UAS, 2026) is a business pilot for a law counselling LLM service.

He has contributed to open source since 2013 under the name paazmaya, with around 9,300 commits across GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket. Several tools have been maintained for over ten years, such as `shigehachi`, `maezato` and `image-flatify`. He also builds web tools and sites for Finnish martial-arts communities, and as director of Design Leaders Finland he makes small, privacy-first apps.
